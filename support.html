Does Day by Day need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your device — adding habits, tracking streaks, notifications, and widgets all work fully offline.

Does the app collect any of my data?

No. There's no account, no sign-up, and no analytics. Your habits and history are stored only in on-device storage and, for the widget, in an App Group container shared between the app and widget — never sent anywhere.

How do I add a new habit?

Tap the round + button in the middle of the tab bar on the Today screen. Give it a name, a color, choose how it's completed (Check, Counter, or Timer), pick which days it's active, and optionally set a daily reminder.

How do reminders work?

Turn on a reminder from a habit's detail screen or when creating it. You'll get a notification at that time on each active day, with two quick actions built in — Done marks the habit complete without opening the app, and In 1 hour reschedules it once.

What do the widgets show?

A small widget with today's progress ring, a medium widget listing today's habits (tap a circle to check one off without opening the app), and Lock Screen widgets for today's progress and your best streak.

Can I use the app in Spanish?

Yes — Day by Day follows your iPhone's language setting automatically, in English or Spanish. Change it under Settings → General → Language & Region.

I found a bug, or have a feature request

Open a ticket on GitHub with what happened and, if it's a bug, the steps to reproduce it. Screenshots help.
