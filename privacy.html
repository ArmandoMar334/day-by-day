Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 30, 2026

In short

Day by Day does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your personal data. There is no account, no analytics, and no server — everything the app knows about your habits stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Day by Day has no backend server and makes no network requests. The app does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or any other personal or usage data, and no data is shared with the developer or any third party.

What's stored, and where

The habits, streaks, and history you create are stored only in local, on-device storage. To let the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show the same data as the app, that storage lives in an App Group container shared between the app and its widget extension — this container never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or anyone else.

Notifications

If you turn on a reminder for a habit, Day by Day schedules a local notification directly on your device using Apple's notification system. No reminder content or schedule is sent anywhere outside your device.

Third parties

Day by Day does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising networks, or crash reporters. No data is shared with third parties because none is collected in the first place.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children either. Day by Day is safe for use by any age for the same reason it's private for everyone: nothing is ever collected.

Deleting your data

Since everything is stored locally, deleting a habit inside the app permanently removes it. Deleting the app from your device removes all remaining data immediately — there is no copy anywhere else to separately request the deletion of.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves network access — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through a ticket on GitHub.
