Day by Day is a quiet, paper-warm habit tracker for iPhone. Check off tasks, count reps, or time a session — with streaks, widgets, and nothing tracked or sent anywhere.

For iPhone · English & Español
Three ways to track

Every habit is different. Your tracker should know that.

Check

Did you do it — yes or no. For habits that don't need a number, like a morning run or lights-out by ten.

Counter

Glasses of water, pages read, push-ups done. Tap up toward a daily target, one unit at a time.

Timer

Minutes meditated, minutes read. For habits measured in time spent, not things counted.

What's inside

Built around the streak, not the checklist.

Streaks that survive a bad day

A 7-day view, a month heatmap, and a running best streak — so one missed day never erases the ones before it.

Widgets & Lock Screen

See today's progress from your Home Screen, check off a habit without opening the app, or glance at your longest streak from the Lock Screen.

Gentle reminders

Optional daily notifications with quick actions — mark a habit done or push it an hour, right from the notification.

Private by design

Nothing leaves your phone.

No account, no sign-up, no analytics. Every habit, streak, and reminder lives only in on-device storage — Day by Day has no server to send it to.
